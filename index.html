AMAN is a revolutionary Lost & Found ecosystem. By assigning unique blockchain-backed QR identities to your valuables, we ensure a secure and rewarded path for their return.

How it Works

The Recovery Journey

Identity Tagging

Print your unique AMAN QR code on any valuable. This code links the physical object to your encrypted owner profile on the blockchain.

Encrypted Chat

Found something? Simply scan the QR code via the AMAN App to open a secure, anonymous chat with the owner without revealing personal data.

Smart Rewards

Owners can declare items as "Lost" and set a bounty. Once the item is safely returned, the AMAN App automatically releases the reward.

Proof of Return (PoR)

Our protocol ensures that the "Finder" receives AMAN Tokens as a reward instantly upon successful verification of the return.
